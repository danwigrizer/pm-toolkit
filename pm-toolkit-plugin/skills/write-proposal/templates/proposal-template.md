## FAQ

### 1. Why did we build this?

[Describe the customer problem in detail. Include:]
- What specific problem were customers experiencing?
- What data or feedback drove this decision? (Include numbers, survey results, support tickets)
- Why did this rise to the top of our priorities?
- What was the cost/impact of NOT solving this?

Example structure:
"We heard from 150+ customers that [problem] was their #1 pain point. They were spending [time/money] on [manual process], which cost them [impact] and prevented them from [strategic goal]. Our data showed that [percentage]% of customers cited this as a barrier to [outcome]."

### 2. Why now?

[Explain the timing. Include:]
- What changed that makes this the right time?
- What market conditions, technology advances, or customer needs aligned?
- Why couldn't we have done this a year ago?
- What window of opportunity exists?

Example structure:
"Three things converged:
1. **Customer demand reached critical mass**: [percentage]% of our customers requested this
2. **Technology matured**: Advances in [technology] made it feasible to build this reliably
3. **Market timing**: Competitors haven't solved this well, giving us a [timeframe] window

We couldn't have built this a year ago because [technical or market constraint]."

### 3. How is this different from [Competitor A] and [Competitor B]?

[Be honest and specific about the competitive landscape. Include:]
- Name actual competitors or alternative solutions
- How does each competitor approach this problem?
- What are the limitations of their approach?
- What's our unique advantage or differentiation?
- Why would customers choose us over them?

Example structure:
"**vs. Competitor A**: They require customers to [limitation] and only work with [scope limit]. We [our approach] and handle [broader scope].

**vs. Competitor B**: They charge per [unit], which gets expensive at scale. We offer [pricing model] that saves customers [percentage]% at typical usage levels.

**Our unique advantage**: We're the only solution that [unique capability], which means customers can [unique outcome they can't get elsewhere]."

### 4. What's the business model?

[Explain how this makes money or creates value. Include:]
- What's the pricing strategy? (Per user, per transaction, subscription, etc.)
- What's the expected ROI for customers?
- What's the revenue potential for us?
- What are the unit economics?
- What's the payback period?

Example structure:
"We charge $[X] per [unit] with a $[Y] minimum. Based on our pilot program:
- Average customer pays $[amount]/month
- Customer saves $[amount]/month in [cost category] ([X]x ROI)
- We expect [percentage]% gross margins
- Payback period for us: [timeframe]
- Projected Year 1 revenue: $[amount]"

### 5. What could go wrong? (Be honest)

[Identify real risks and be transparent. Include:]
- What are the biggest risks to success?
- What assumptions might be wrong?
- What could cause this to fail?
- What would be early warning signals?
- How would we mitigate or respond?

Example structure:
"**Risk #1: [Risk name]**
- **What it is**: [Description of the risk]
- **Impact if it happens**: [Consequence]
- **Likelihood**: [High/Medium/Low]
- **Early signal**: [How we'd know it's happening]
- **Mitigation**: [What we'd do to prevent or respond]

**Risk #2: [Risk name]**
- [Same structure]

**Risk #3: [Risk name]**
- [Same structure]"

### 6. What's the long-term vision?

[Paint the picture of success. Include:]
- If this succeeds beyond expectations, what becomes possible?
- What's the 3-5 year vision?
- How does this expand or evolve?
- What adjacent opportunities does this unlock?
- How does this position us strategically?

Example structure:
"If this succeeds, we become the platform for [broader category]. In 3-5 years:
- Customers use us for [expanded use case 1], [2], and [3]
- We enable [new ecosystem, marketplace, or platform play]
- We expand from [current market] to [adjacent markets]
- We process/handle [scale metric] per day, up from [current metric]
- This positions us to [strategic outcome or market leadership position]"

### 7. Who is this NOT for?

[Set clear boundaries. Include:]
- What customer segments won't benefit from this?
- What use cases are explicitly out of scope?
- When should customers use alternatives instead?
- What limitations or constraints exist?

Example structure:
"**Not for [segment]**: [Why they wouldn't benefit and what they should use instead]

**Not for [use case]**: [Why this isn't the right solution and what the alternative is]

**Not for [scenario]**: [Limitation and when to use something else]

**Not if [constraint]**: [Technical or business constraint that makes this unsuitable]"

## Writing Checklist

Before finalizing, ensure:

### Press Release
- [ ] Headline is 10 words or less and compelling
- [ ] Subheadline is 20-30 words clarifying what and who
- [ ] Customer quote includes specific, measurable outcomes (not "improved efficiency")
- [ ] "How It Works" focuses on outcomes, not features
- [ ] Total length is 1 page or less
- [ ] Language is customer-friendly (readable by journalist)
- [ ] No vague language ("improve," "optimize," "better")

### FAQ
- [ ] All 7 questions are answered
- [ ] FAQ #1 includes data and numbers backing the problem
- [ ] FAQ #2 explains why now with specific triggers
- [ ] FAQ #3 names actual competitors and honestly compares
- [ ] FAQ #4 includes specific pricing and ROI numbers
- [ ] FAQ #5 is honest about risks with mitigation plans
- [ ] FAQ #6 paints an inspiring but realistic long-term vision
- [ ] FAQ #7 sets clear boundaries on who this ISN'T for
- [ ] Total length is 1 page or less
- [ ] All outcomes are concrete and measurable
- [ ] No jargon or internal terminology
